Privacy Policy — Vital ad campaign

What we collect when you see a Vital ad or leave your details to book a demo, and what we do with it.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information is collected when you see a Vital ad on Google or YouTube, or leave your details to book a demo, and what is done with it.

It applies to:

  • Vital’s video and search ads on Google and YouTube.
  • The demo request form inside the ad.
  • Anything you send us as a result of the campaign, including by email.

It does not cover the Vital app or the information patients enter into it. Use of the app is governed by a separate privacy policy.

The campaign is aimed at nutrition and health professionals and clinics. We do not ask for any medical information.

2. Who we are

The information is collected and managed by the Vital project (“Vital”, “we” or “us”). For privacy questions, write to danielpl0908@gmail.com.

3. What we collect

You are under no legal obligation to give us any information, but without your name and a way to reach you we cannot schedule a demo with you.

Type of informationExamplesWhere it comes from
Contact and work details Name, phone number, role, and company or clinic name (optional) You, through the demo request form in the ad
Interaction with the ad Which ad was shown, how much of the video was watched, clicks Google — mostly as aggregated data that does not identify you
Correspondence with you The content of your messages, the demo date, a summary of the call You and us

When you fill in a lead form inside a Google ad, Google may pre-fill some fields from your Google account. Your details only reach us after you tap submit.

The campaign’s form lives inside the ad itself, so we do not place cookies or tracking tags of our own as part of it.

4. Why we use the information

PurposeBasis
Getting back to you, scheduling and holding a demo, and answering your questionsYour request
Measuring which ads work and improving the campaignAggregated data from Google
Sending updates and marketing contentSeparate, explicit consent (section 9)
Complying with the law and preventing abuseLegal obligation and legitimate interest

We do not make automated decisions about you. Google uses algorithms to decide who is shown ads, according to the settings in your Google account.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not pass your details on to other professionals or clinics. The information only goes to:

  • Google (Google Ads, Google Analytics, YouTube): to show and measure the ads, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Service providers working for us: email, managing enquiries and scheduling meetings. They use the information only on our instructions and are bound to keep it secure.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it or under a court order.
  • A buyer or successor: if Vital’s activity is transferred to another entity, subject to this policy.

6. Transfers outside Israel

Some of the information is stored on servers outside Israel, mainly in the United States and the European Union, with Google and our service providers. Transfers are made under the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761–2001, and on the basis of the providers’ contractual commitments to protect the information. You can ask us for a copy of those commitments.

7. How long we keep it, and how it is secured

Form details and correspondence are kept for up to 24 months from our last contact with you. If you become a customer, the information will be kept according to our agreement with you. Ad viewing data is kept by Google under its own policy.

We secure the information in line with the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777–2017: encryption in transit (HTTPS), access limited to the people who need it, and providers who are bound to keep it secure. If a serious security incident occurs, we will report it to the authority and to you as the law requires.

8. Your rights

To exercise any of these rights, write to danielpl0908@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity.

  • Access: receive the information we hold about you (section 13 of the Protection of Privacy Law).
  • Correction or deletion: of information that is wrong, incomplete or out of date (section 14).
  • Opting out of direct marketing: at any time, with no need to give a reason.
  • Withdrawing consent to mailings: at any time, without affecting anything done before.
  • Personalised ads: you can turn these off in Google’s My Ad Center.
  • Complaints: to the Privacy Protection Authority, if you believe we have not handled your request properly.

If you are in the European Union, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including data portability and the right to complain to the privacy authority in your country.

9. Marketing messages

We will only send you marketing email, SMS or other marketing messages if you have ticked a box agreeing to it, as section 30A of the Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law requires. The box is not pre-ticked, and agreeing is not a condition for booking a demo.

Every marketing message includes a simple way to unsubscribe. You can also reply “STOP” or write to us.

Messages about a demo you asked for, such as scheduling or a reminder, are not marketing and will be sent even without this consent.

10. Health information and minors

Please do not send medical information about yourself or your patients through the form. If such information reaches us by mistake, we will delete it.

The campaign is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about minors.

11. Changes to this policy, and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is material, we will let everyone who left their details know by email.

Questions and requests: danielpl0908@gmail.com
