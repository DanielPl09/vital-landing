Personalized intake, under three minutes

Vital decides which standard assessments a patient actually needs, then runs those in full. Most answers are a tap, not a paragraph — so the intake finishes in one sitting and every score still scores.

02

From invite to booked, in one sitting

Chat reaches patients where email doesn’t. The gap between reaching out and getting an answer collapses from days to minutes — and the first session starts warm instead of starting from zero.

03

Priority you can actually read

One transparent formula weighs how long someone has waited against what their intake says. Tune it, or write your own — and every booking still waits on a clinician’s approval.

04

It meets a thousand patients before it meets yours

Every build is rehearsed against simulated patients — including the difficult ones. Steering, refusals and escalation are proven there, not in your waiting room.
