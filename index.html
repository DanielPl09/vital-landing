Features

Three things your clinic gets on day one

Intake that reads like a conversation

New patients answer in their own words — history, goals, habits, consent. Vital runs your intake and questionnaires as a chat, then files each answer back to the patient record as a structured field. Your dietitian opens a finished picture instead of an empty form.

  • Intake and history captured in plain language
  • Free-text answers mapped to the fields your clinic already tracks
  • Follows up until nothing required is left blank

You choose what it’s allowed to raise

Sleep, training load, medical history, eating patterns — switch a topic on and it enters the conversation; switch it off and Vital never brings it up. The question set belongs to your clinic, and a change applies to the next conversation rather than disturbing one already under way.

  • Pick topics from your clinic’s own list — no prompt writing
  • Off means out of scope, not merely discouraged
  • Every patient gets the set you approved, whoever is on shift

The calendar fills itself — you approve it

When a patient is ready to book, Vital offers real openings from your clinic’s availability and takes the slot inside the thread. Every booking arrives as a request waiting on a clinician, so nothing lands in the diary unapproved.

  • Offers only the slots your calendar actually has free
  • Booked in the conversation, before the thread goes cold
  • Confirmations and reschedules sync back to your clinic calendar

Safety & compliance

Held to a standard your clinicians can defend

Vital stays inside the boundary you draw

Red-flag language, out-of-scope medical questions and distress signals are escalated to a human dietitian in seconds. Every exchange is logged, reviewable and exportable for your records.

  • Red-flag detection with instant human handover
  • Hard limits on diagnosis and medication advice
  • Full audit trail on every conversation

HIPAA

PHI encrypted in transit and at rest, role-based access, BAA available for every clinic.

GDPR

EU data residency, consent captured in-chat, export and erasure on request.
