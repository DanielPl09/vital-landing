Features

Three things your clinic gets on day one

Built for dietitian practices — the clinic sets the protocol, Vital does the follow-through.

New patients are onboarded the moment they land — intake questions, history, goals, consent. From the same thread they book, move or confirm an appointment, and it lands straight in your clinic calendar. No forms to chase, no phone tag.

  • Guided intake and history in a conversation
  • Appointment booking and rescheduling in-thread
  • Every message written back to the patient record

The moment a patient is ready to book, Vital checks your real availability and proposes the next open slot — no coordinator in the loop. Confirmations, reschedules and cancellations sync straight back to the calendar your clinic already runs on.

  • Live sync with your clinic calendar and availability rules
  • Automatic confirmations, reminders and reschedule requests
  • Cancelled slots are re-offered to the next patient on the waitlist

Vital stays inside the boundary you draw. Red-flag language, out-of-scope medical questions and distress signals are escalated to a human dietitian in seconds, and every exchange is logged, reviewable and exportable for your records.

  • Red-flag detection with instant human handover
  • Hard limits on diagnosis and medication advice
  • Full audit trail on every conversation
